# Marketing DentalFlow AI to clinics

DentalFlow AI is a front-desk workspace for a dental clinic. It keeps calls, leads, and appointments in one place, calls missed callers back by voice, and shows the owner what happened.

Use this note when talking to a clinic owner or office manager. Describe only what the product does today.

## Who it is for

Independent clinics with one front desk and a few dentists. The buyer is the owner or the office manager. The daily user is the person who answers the phone.

The pain to open with: callers who hang up when nobody answers, and the follow-up that never happens after a busy morning.

## What to show

Walk through Maple Grove as the example clinic, using Demo Mode first so no real patient is dialed.

1. Dashboard. Show call volume, the missed-call card, and the business insights. Ask a question with **Ask AI** and read the answer from those insights.
2. Calls. Log a finished call and open the record. Point out that a missed inbound call is queued for one voice callback.
3. Campaigns. Show a scheduled voice call, including a spreadsheet of names and times. The clinic chooses who gets called.
4. Knowledge base. Ask a question that is answered from the clinic's own FAQ, such as hours, parking, or a cancellation policy.
5. Leads and appointments. Show an inquiry moving toward a booked visit.
6. Reports. Generate a daily or monthly summary and download the PDF.

Then switch to Live Mode only with a staff login the clinic controls.

## What to say it does

- Staff sign in, and sign out ends that session.
- The owner sees calls, leads, appointments, and a short read of the last 30 days.
- Missed inbound callers can be called back by the AI receptionist. The clinic can pause that campaign.
- Outbound calls are scheduled one at a time or from a spreadsheet.
- Clinic documents stay in the clinic's database. Questions are answered from those files.
- A report can be downloaded or opened in the manager's own email program.

## What not to promise

- DentalFlow does not send text messages or marketing email.
- It does not connect to Google Calendar, take payments, or request reviews.
- It does not diagnose, prescribe, or invent fees. Prices in an answer have to come from a document the clinic uploaded.
- Saving receptionist rules in the app does not change the voice agent until that agent is updated separately.
- A scheduled call goes out only while the app is open, or while the call dispatcher is active in n8n.

## How to open the conversation

Lead with the missed call, not the software.

"When the front desk is with a patient, the next caller often hangs up. DentalFlow calls that person back and keeps the inquiry on a board the owner can see."

One unpaid implant consult is often worth more than a month of front-desk software. Use the clinic's own fees if they share them. Do not quote another clinic's numbers.

## A simple offer

1. A 30-minute visit at the clinic, on their front-desk computer.
2. Demo Mode for the tour, so nothing dials a patient.
3. A two-week pilot on a staff login, with one agreed callback list. The clinic writes the names. DentalFlow does not buy a list.
4. A weekly PDF report the owner can read in a few minutes.

Price it as a monthly fee for the clinic, plus the cost of the voice minutes. Say the voice cost out loud. Do not hide it inside the software fee.

## Where to find clinics

- The owner's own patients who are dentists, and their study club.
- A local dental society meeting. Offer the 30-minute front-desk tour, not a booth speech.
- Office managers on the same street or plaza. One clinic that will take a reference call is more useful than a large ad.
- A short screen recording of the dashboard and a callback, with fictional names.

Skip purchased lead lists and cold texting. The product itself does not text.

## Objections

**We already have a receptionist.** DentalFlow covers the calls that arrive while that person is with a patient, and the callbacks they do not have time to make.

**We do not want an AI talking to patients.** The clinic writes the rules, chooses who gets a callback, and can pause the recovery campaign. Staff can still log and return calls themselves.

**Where does patient information go?** It stays in the clinic's Supabase project. Staff reach it only after they sign in. Signing out closes that session.

**Will it give clinical advice?** No. It books and follows up. It does not diagnose or promise a price that is not in the clinic's documents.
